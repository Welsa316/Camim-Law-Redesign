## 3. Photography direction

One session covers items 4 through 9.

- **Treatment.** Full colour, natural light, no brand tint, no duotone, no grayscale on the primary portrait. Tinting a real person's face reads as a filter applied to them.
- **Setting.** The actual office. Not a rented studio, not a composited background. The existing white-ground portrait already gives the clean option; what is missing is place.
- **Framing.** Consistent rules across the set: the same eye line, the same depth of field, the same tonal target, so the images read as one body of work rather than a collection.
- **Wardrobe.** The bow tie and glasses are already his visual signature across the portrait, the banner and all three television appearances. That consistency is an asset and should be kept, not styled away.
- **Mobile crops.** Every hero image needs an art-directed portrait crop, not a centre crop. The current banner is the cautionary example: at 390px the automatic crop destroys it.
- **What not to shoot.** No gavels, no scales, no flags, no law-book walls, no handshakes, no pointing at a laptop, no staged client meetings. Rule 4-7.15(a) additionally bans emotionally manipulative scenes, which rules out anything staging family separation.

## 4. If photography is not available

In priority order, with the honest cost of each:

1. **The existing studio portrait, used large and well.** Fully viable for launch. The homepage opener becomes a composition of type and one excellent portrait on paper ground. This is a real design, not a fallback, and it is what the site launches with if nothing else arrives.
2. **The Telemundo still**, presented factually as a media appearance.
3. **Reel stills**, cropped and captioned, as evidence of the teaching he already does.
4. **Type-led sections with no imagery.** For service pages, a well-set page with a rule, a form number and a clear hierarchy beats a stock photograph of a pen. The best-in-class research is unambiguous that stock imagery is the fastest way to look like a template.
5. **Abstract or generated imagery** is acceptable only for non-credibility surfaces such as an article header, and never for anything depicting a person. No generated faces anywhere, ever.

## 5. Delivery specification

- Formats: AVIF with a WebP fallback and a JPEG last resort.
- Widths: 400, 800, 1200, 1600, 2400 for full-bleed. `srcset` and `sizes` matched to the actual layout.
- Every image carries explicit `width` and `height` so nothing shifts during load, which is the main lever on Cumulative Layout Shift.
- `loading="eager"` and `fetchpriority="high"` on the opener image only; everything else lazy.
- Alt text describes the person or the scene, in the page's language, and never carries keyword padding.
- The opener image is the Largest Contentful Paint element and is budgeted at 180KB after compression.
