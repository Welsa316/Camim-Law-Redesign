# Needs Attorney Verification Before Launch

Research, not a legal opinion. Every suggested wording is a draft for the attorney to accept, modify or reject. Rule citations are to the Rules Regulating The Florida Bar, Chapter 4, June 30 2026 compilation, and to the Handbook on Lawyer Advertising and Solicitation, 13th edition, effective December 10 2025.

Status column: **built** means the site already does this; **confirm** means it needs a decision; **hold** means it is deliberately absent pending an answer.

## Ten phrases to keep off the site

1. "Best immigration lawyer in Orlando", "#1", "top-rated" — 4-7.13(b)(3)
2. "We guarantee approval", "we will get your green card" — 4-7.13(b)(1)
3. Any win rate or approval percentage — 4-7.13(b)(1)-(2)
4. "Immigration specialists", "we specialize in immigration" — 4-7.14(a)(5)-(6)
5. "Board certified" — 4-7.14(a)(4)
6. "Licensed in all 50 states", "offices nationwide" — 4-7.13(a)(3)
7. "Notario", "notaría", "consultor de inmigración" — §§ 117.05(11), 501.1391
8. "Over 20 years of experience" combining lawyers or non-lawyer years — 4-7.13(a)(2)
9. Unlabelled award badges, or "prestigious" — 4-7.14(a)(3)
10. "Florida Bar approved", or any Bar or government seal — 4-7.13(b)(10), 4-7.21(b)
