Phase 03

Video and reviews

The two things the firm already owns that the current site does nothing with: eleven Spanish explainer videos, and a five-star review record.

  • —
    The eleven Spanish videosHosting decided, poster frames, captions, and each video placed on the practice-area page it explains. They run from 17 to 64 MB, so where they are hosted is a decision before they ship.
  • —
    The broadcast segmentsTelemundo, Univision and TVV made playable where the footage allows, rather than stills.
  • —
    Google reviews on the siteThe live rating and a selection of reviews, updating on their own rather than being retyped.
  • —
    TranscriptsEach video's Spanish transcript on the page, which is readable for anyone who cannot play sound and is the part search engines can actually read.

Before phase 02

What I need from you

Phase two can start as soon as these are settled. Four of the five are readings or decisions rather than work.

  • The admission wordingThe site states your Louisiana admission because Campos Muños Law publishes that sentence. It does not yet say that you are not admitted in Florida, or that immigration is federal practice, because no published source says it and nothing is written here. Those sentences need to come from you.
  • Where enquiries should goAn inbox for the consultation form.
  • The fourteen practice-area pagesThey describe federal processes, so they carry over — but special immigrant juvenile status turns on state court practice, and that page in particular needs your read against Florida.
  • The photographsConfirmation that the portraits may be used, whether the photographer needs a credit, and the full-resolution original of the headshot if you have it.
  • Where the videos will liveNeeded before phase three, useful to decide early.

The preview of phase one is at preview link — the two finished pages, in both languages. Every other link on them is deliberately inert, so nothing unreviewed is reachable.

Phases three and four are separated on purpose. Video and reviews are evidence; chat and Instagram are conversation. Each is worth doing properly on its own rather than shipping four integrations in one release.
